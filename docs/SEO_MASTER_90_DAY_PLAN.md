Version: 1.0 | 10 November 2026

## Decision: build now, migrate later
- Current: GitHub Pages, deployed with GitHub Actions at https://brionestsolutions.github.io/BrionestWebsite/
- Future official domain: https://brionest.in/
- Do NOT change DNS, canonical URLs, or the BRIONEST_SITE_URL variable prematurely. The static build already generates canonical tags and sitemap URLs for the actually deployed hostname, and can switch base URLs when the new domain goes live.
- Build pages with durable URL paths that remain the same after the later migration. Do not launch mass backlink campaigns to the temporary GitHub address.
- Before migration, inventory all old brionest.in URLs and backlinks; map 1:1 redirects to relevant new URLs; preserve email DNS and the separately hosted consultation backend.

## Growth philosophy
Our north star is QUALIFIED INBOUND ENQUIRIES, not article count or pageviews. Use two acquisition funnels:

A. HOMEOWNERS | 60% of initial content effort
- Trigger: possession / moving into a new flat, planning interiors, new electrical work, or considering a home retrofit.
- Need: compatible devices, installation advice, convenience, safety, realistic budgets and lasting support.
- Positioning: "Your new home is getting beautiful. Make it intelligent too."
- Primary conversion: Plan My Smart Home. Form captures home stage, automation priority and timeline.
- Proof: genuine device demos, electrical/interior planning checklist, approved installation images, real reviews, price scope and warranty details.

B. BUILDERS / DEVELOPERS | 40% of initial content effort
- Trigger: a project in design/approvals, construction, electrical rough-in, sample-flat planning or sales launch.
- Need: cost-controlled baseline smart features, a visible model-flat experience, unit-wise scope, deployment and buyer handover.
- Positioning: "Give buyers one more reason to choose your project."
- Primary conversion: Request a Smart-Ready Proposal. Form captures construction stage and approximate units.
- Proof: unit specification matrix, real demonstration, commissioning workflow, buyer training and factual project execution.
- Never guarantee faster inventory sales, higher selling price, profit uplift or referral commission without confirmed evidence and an approved commercial offer.

## 4. Organic distribution: one expert insight, many channels
- Google Search: pillar pages + high-intent articles + accurate schema/canonicals/sitemap.
- Google Business Profile: only an eligible real business location/service-area profile. Use legitimate service area, working hours, verified contact and authentic job images. Do not fabricate branch locations.
- YouTube Shorts + Instagram: 30–60 second demos of a real device or workflow; answer one homeowner question; link to matching pillar page.
- LinkedIn: project architects, electrical consultants, real estate owners and builder sales teams; post authentic model-flat scopes and handover checklists. Avoid automated spam.
- Partnerships: co-create checklists with interior designers, electricians and architects, publish genuine referrals and earned links; no paid link schemes.
- Content flywheel: technical enquiry -> answer/article -> real demo -> social snippets -> visitor -> consultation -> approved case study -> next topic.

## 5. Technical quality gate for GitHub Actions
Existing SEO build generates metadata, canonicals, sitemap and robots. For each new page:
1. Distinct static HTML path, correct heading, descriptive title and meta description; no duplicate/low-value pages.
2. Build-script registry update plus deployment copy and sitemap/test update.
3. Self canonical points at CURRENT working URL; later switch to brionest.in only after cutover.
4. Internal links both directions between the guide, its main pillar, and a clear CTA.
5. Structured data matches visible text. Do not add fake ratings or "special AI SEO" markup.
6. Responsive desktop and mobile screenshot test; 320px minimum; no sideways scrolling.
7. Check production image loading, alt text, descriptive links and minimal external assets.
8. Validate LCP <=2.5s, INP <=200ms, CLS <=0.1 at p75 once field data exists. Lab findings should be labelled as lab data, not real-user scores.
9. Contact form must truly deliver into Google Sheets before paid or high-volume distribution.
10. Avoid deploying tracking scripts unless privacy and consent requirements are addressed.

## 6. Leads and measurement
North-star KPIs: qualified homeowner consultations, qualified builder project leads, meetings, scoped quotations, wins and value of pipeline.

Search Console (weekly): non-branded clicks, impressions, CTR, indexed pages, highest-intent queries, landing page trends.
GA4 (once configured): landing page, acquisition source, lead-confirmed key event (only after actual backend save), click-to-call/email and package interest. DO NOT send name, email, phone or free-text details in analytics.
Sales (weekly): enquiries by audience, genuine qualification, project/unit count, follow-up completed, proposal issued, project won.

First 90 days: operational goals, NOT fictional traffic predictions. Deliver 8–10 expert-reviewed pages/checklists, a repeatable video process, working analytics and search ownership, and a reliable sales-to-content feedback loop. Establish real traffic/lead targets from the first 30–60 days of measured data.

5. Confirm new self-canonicals, sitemap and internal links; configure permanent redirects where supported and inspect old GitHub Pages project paths.
6. Verify Google Search Console properties; submit new sitemap and inspect priority pages. Monitor both URL sets and preserve redirects for at least 12 months.
7. Update social/GBP/partner references to the canonical domain after launch.
