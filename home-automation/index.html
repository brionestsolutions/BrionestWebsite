Plan the experience before the finishing touches.

01

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Should I plan automation before interior work?

It helps to consider switches, available power, connectivity and control locations while the layout is flexible. The exact installation approach depends on your property and chosen products.

Can I automate an existing home?

Often, yes. Retrofit feasibility depends on the wiring, device compatibility, network coverage and desired functions. BrioNest can assess suitable options.

Do I need to automate my entire home?

No. You can begin with useful rooms or a few high-value functions, then expand with compatible products later.

Is the ₹20,000 package the final price?

It is an indicative package from the earlier BrioNest website. A confirmed quotation should specify devices, installation conditions, inclusions and support.
